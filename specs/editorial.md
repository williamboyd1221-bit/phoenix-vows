# Phoenix Vows editorial spec

A working spec for a wedding brand that reads like a well-made printed piece. All sample copy is **example copy**. There are no real couples, vendors or prices here.

## Tone

- Warm, plain and specific. Say what happens and when.
- First person plural for a business ("we"), second person for the couple ("you").
- Short sentences. One idea per line.
- No hype words, no pressure, no invented praise.
- Never show a price in marketing copy. The call to action is: **Call 541-632-9043 or book a Business X-Ray.**

## Layout grid

- **12-column grid** on desktop, 4-column on phones.
- Generous margins: at least 8% of page width on each side.
- One main image per spread, with one block of text beside or below it.
- Headlines left-aligned. Captions small, set in italic.
- Thin ink rules (1px) to separate sections. White space does most of the work.
- Reading width: about 60 to 70 characters per line.

## Photography direction

- Natural light. Soft shadows. Real moments over posed ones.
- Hands, details, places: a ring on a table, a folded program, a doorway.
- Warm, slightly muted color. Film-like grain is fine; heavy filters are not.
- Wide, medium and detail frames in every set, so the story has pacing.
- Only use photos that the business owns or has permission to use. Credit the photographer.
- No stock photos presented as real weddings, and no invented galleries.

## Palette

| Role | Name | Hex |
|------|------|-----|
| Background | Paper | `#f4efe6` |
| Text | Ink | `#12100e` |
| Accent (sparingly) | Ember | `#e36a2e` |
| Soft line | Warm gray | `#d9d2c5` |
| Quiet text | Faded ink | `#5a534b` |

No gold, purple or neon.

## Type

- **Headlines:** Newsreader serif, regular or italic, generous size.
- **Body:** Newsreader at a readable size, or a clean sans-serif for small UI text.
- **Details:** small caps or letter-spaced uppercase for dates and labels, used lightly.
- **Mark:** a small circular monogram.

## Sample headline structures (EXAMPLES)

| Structure | Example |
|-----------|---------|
| A quiet promise | "A day that feels like you." |
| Place and season | "Autumn in [town], planned slowly." |
| A question | "What do you want to remember?" |
| Plain how-it-works | "Tell us the story. We help you tell it well." |
| Invitation | "Come see how it comes together." |

The words in brackets are placeholders. Every headline here is an example.

## Checklist before anything is published

- [ ] A person has read and approved it
- [ ] No real names, reviews or prices without written permission
- [ ] Photos are owned or licensed
- [ ] Every link was tested
- [ ] Call to action present, no price
